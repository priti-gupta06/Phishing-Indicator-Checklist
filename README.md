# Phishing-Indicator-Checklist
analyze sample safe phishing emails and identify suspicious indicators


## Objective :  
The objective of this task is to identify common phishing indicators and develop basic phishing detection skills.

## Phishing Indicator Checklist :

- Sender Address : Check for unknown senders, fake email addresses, and misspelled domains.
- Suspicious Links : Check URLs before clicking and look for shortened or unusual links.
- Urgency : Be careful with messages creating panic, threats, or immediate action.
- Attachments : Avoid opening unexpected or suspicious attachments.
- Email Content : Look for spelling mistakes, unusual wording, and generic greetings.
- Sensitive Information : Be cautious of requests for passwords, OTPs, PINs, or banking details. 

## Safe Actions :

- Do not click suspicious links.
- Do not open unexpected attachments.
- Do not share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or banking information.
- Verify suspicious emails through an official source.
- Report suspected phishing emails to the IT or security team.

## Conclusion :

Phishing emails often use fake sender addresses, suspicious links, urgency, unexpected attachments, and requests for sensitive information. 
Checking these indicators can help identify phishing attempts and improve email security awareness.
